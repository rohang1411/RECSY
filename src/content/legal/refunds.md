### 2. Query Credit Packs

2.1. **Unused Credit Packs:** If you purchase a query credit pack (e.g., Pack S with 25 credits, or Pack M with 100 credits) and have not used any credits from that pack, you may request a full refund within **14 calendar days** of the purchase date.

2.2. **Partially Used Packs:** Once query credits from a purchased pack have been consumed, the pack is generally non-refundable. If you experienced documented technical defects or service outages, we may offer a pro-rata refund or credit adjustment at our discretion.

2.3. **No Credit Expiration:** Unused credits remain valid in your account balance indefinitely while your account remains active and in good standing.

---

### 3. Pro Monthly Subscriptions

3.1. **Cancellation Anytime:** You may cancel your Pro monthly subscription at any time via the Customer Portal (`/account/billing` or `/api/billing/portal`).

3.2. **Access Through Period End:** Upon cancellation, your Pro benefits (150 monthly queries, deep reasoning mode, cross-device history) remain fully active until the end of your current paid billing period (`entitled_until`). You will not be billed again.

3.3. **Statutory Cooling-Off Rights:** In jurisdictions where statutory rights of withdrawal apply (such as the European Union and United Kingdom), you may cancel a new subscription within **14 days** of initial activation for a full refund, provided you have not extensively utilized paid queries during that initial window.

---

### 4. Technical Failures & Double Charges

If an unexpected technical error or network interruption results in:

- A double charge or duplicate transaction on your card, or
- Deducted credits without delivering a response from the recommendation pipeline,

our system's automated lease settling mechanism will typically refund the query automatically. If a manual correction is required, contact our support team with your transaction reference. Verified duplicate charges will be refunded in full immediately.

---

### 5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

1. Email `support@recsy.app` from the email address linked to your RECSY account.
2. Include your **Order ID** (found on your receipt or in `/account/billing`), the date of purchase, and a brief description of the issue.
3. We review all requests within **24 to 48 business hours**. Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days depending on your financial institution.
