# Contact & Support

We are dedicated to providing clear, responsive support for RECSY users, partners, and security researchers.

---

### 1. General & Billing Support

For assistance with your account, credit packs, subscription billing, or recommendation queries:

- **Email:** `support@recsy.app`
- **Response Commitment:** We review and respond to inquiries within **24 to 48 business hours** (Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays).
- **Self-Service:** You can view real-time query consumption, download invoices, or cancel subscriptions directly in your [Account Dashboard](/account).

---

### 2. Privacy & Data Protection

To exercise your GDPR/CCPA data rights, request account deletion, or discuss personal data management:

- **Email:** `privacy@recsy.app`
- **Data Export:** You can instantly download your complete data archive at any time from your Account settings.

---

### 3. Vulnerability Reporting & Security

If you believe you have identified a security vulnerability in our web application, API, or infrastructure, we appreciate prompt disclosure:

- **Email:** `security@recsy.app`
- Please include reproducible steps, request traces, and relevant payloads. We commit to acknowledging reports within 24 hours.
